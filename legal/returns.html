Returns & Exchanges

Your satisfaction is our priority - hassle-free returns guaranteed

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you're not happy with your luxury item, we offer a comprehensive return and exchange policy designed with your peace of mind in mind.

30-Day Returns

Standard Members

30 days from delivery to initiate a return for unworn, unused items in original condition with all tags and packaging.

VIP Benefit

60-Day Returns

VIP Members

Extended 60-day return window with priority processing and complimentary return shipping both ways.

Free Exchanges

All Members

Exchange for a different size, color, or item with no additional fees. We cover shipping both ways.

Full Refund

All Members

Receive a full refund to your original payment method within 5-7 business days of receiving your return.

How to Return an Item

1

Contact Us

Reach out to our customer service team to initiate your return and receive a return authorization number

2

Package Your Item

Carefully package your item in its original packaging with all accessories, tags, and documentation

3

Ship Your Return

Use the prepaid return label we provide (VIP) or ship via insured carrier with tracking

4

Receive Your Refund

Once we receive and inspect your return, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days

Return Requirements

✓

Unworn & Unused

Item must be in original, pristine condition

✓

Original Packaging

All tags, labels, and packaging included

✓

Documentation

Certificate of authenticity must be returned

✓

Within Window

Return initiated within applicable timeframe

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Personalized or custom-made items
  • Items marked as final sale
  • Worn or altered items
  • Items without original packaging or tags

Need to Return an Item?

Our customer service team is here to make your return process as smooth as possible.
