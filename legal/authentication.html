Authentication Guarantee

Every piece is verified for authenticity by certified experts

At Kuraj Fashion, authenticity is non-negotiable. Every luxury item that enters our collection undergoes a rigorous multi-point authentication process conducted by certified experts with decades of experience in luxury goods verification.

01

Expert Verification

Our team of certified authenticators examines every detail, from materials and craftsmanship to serial numbers and brand-specific markers.

02

Certificate of Authenticity

Each item comes with a detailed certificate of authenticity, documenting its verification and providing peace of mind.

03

Lifetime Guarantee

We stand behind every piece with a lifetime authenticity guarantee. If any item is found to be inauthentic, we offer a full refund.

04

Advanced Technology

We utilize cutting-edge authentication technology including microscopic analysis and material testing to ensure accuracy.

What We Verify

✓ Brand markings, logos, and engravings
✓ Serial numbers and date codes
✓ Material quality and composition
✓ Stitching patterns and craftsmanship
✓ Hardware and component authenticity
✓ Packaging and documentation
✓ Provenance and chain of custody
✓ Age and wear patterns analysis

Our authentication experts are here to answer any questions you may have about our verification process.
